Fahsai — Thailand Air Quality Map

Fahsai (ฟ้าใส, “clear sky”) is a free, non-commercial map of the causes of seasonal air pollution in Thailand and its neighbours. It shows satellite fire detections, PM2.5 readings from ground stations, gridded PM2.5 estimates, wind patterns and power plants on one map, updated daily.

It covers Thailand, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam and Singapore, with up to 120 days of history, so you can see how fires, wind and air quality change together through the haze season.
